How it works

1

Connect your channel

Log in with Twitch and you're live. Clicktide listens for gift subs, resubs, bits and channel point redemptions — automatically, on every stream.

2

Fans hit milestones

10 gift subs in a month. A 12-month anniversary. You choose the rules, the rewards and a monthly budget — Clicktide never overspends it.

3

Real mail shows up

The fan gets a private claim link, enters their address once, and a printed card lands in their mailbox — from you, with their stats on it.
